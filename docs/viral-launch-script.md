# Viral Launch Video Script

## Structure (60-90 seconds)

---

### HOOK (0-5 sec)

> "What if your robot could see the world like this?"

*[Show demo GIF - person following or object tracking]*

---

### PROBLEM (5-15 sec)

> "Most robots are blind. They react, but they never predict."

*[Quick montage: robot bumping into things, failing to track]*

---

### SOLUTION (15-30 sec)

> "Meet OpenEyes."

*[Logo animation + quick feature montage]*

> "The open-source vision framework that gives robots real eyes."

*[Show detection + tracking + depth all running]*

---

### CAPABILITIES (30-50 sec)

> "Object detection. Pose estimation. Face tracking. Depth sensing."

*[Each feature flashes by]*

> "All running at 30+ FPS on a $199 Jetson."

*[Show FPS counter on screen]*

> "With ROS2 integration, Docker deployment, and TensorRT optimization — out of the box."

*[Quick: CLI commands, Docker running, ROS2 topics]*

---

### WHO IS IT FOR (50-65 sec)

> "For researchers building the next generation of humanoid robots."

> "For companies deploying at scale."

> "For makers who want real computer vision — not just tutorials."

*[Mix of lab, warehouse, hobbyist setups]*

---

### CTA (65-80 sec)

> "OpenEyes is free. Open-source. And ready to run."

*[Star count, GitHub link]*

> "Build the future. Give your robot vision."

*[End card: GitHub link + Discord link]*

---

## Viral Tips

| Element | Why |
|:--------|:---|
| **Start with demo, not text** | GIFs show, words tell |
| **Fast pace** | First 10 seconds = hook or scroll |
| **Show real hardware** | Jetson = tangible, aspirational |
| **Specific numbers** | "30+ FPS on $199" = credibility |
| **Emotional beat** | "Give your robot vision" = mission |
